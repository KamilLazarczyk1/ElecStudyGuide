How to use this guide

1. Study in order. The six sections build on each other — AC circuits assume you're fluent in the DC tools, and resonance assumes reactance. Tick the checkbox next to each topic in the sidebar as you master it; your progress is saved in the browser and shows up on the cards above.

2. Read the figures, not just the text. Each diagram is taken from the actual lecture slides — if it's here, your professor considered it important enough to show. Click any figure to zoom.

3. Drill formulas with the cheat sheet. Use the search box ("divider", "τ", "RMS"…) and quiz yourself: cover the variables list, read the formula, and recite what each symbol means and its unit.

4. Finish with the quiz. It reuses the lecture's own numerical examples. Anything you miss links back to a topic — reread it, then retake.
